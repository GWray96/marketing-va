# Strategy Foundation — Marketing Executive VA

> Playbook Part 1 outputs, locked 2026-06-11. Every page is graded against this document.
> Naming decision (confirmed by founder): **"Marketing Executive VA"** is the category name — used in `<title>`, H1, schema. Because "VA" can undersell seniority, the copy system carries the counterweight everywhere: pair the name with "your whole marketing department, one person" and lead with seniority + outcomes (the implicit objection gets the CO-only treatment — never name the fear).

## 1.1 Positioning → emotional quadrant

**Warm + Active.** Peer-guide with momentum: "this is exciting AND friendly", "finally someone's handling it." Energetic, direct, reassuring. Design direction: "Copper Momentum" (see research/design-report.md) — the whole agency category sits Cold+Active; warm editorial + founder-forward is the open territory.

## 1.2 ICP + awareness stage

- **One persona:** owner/founder of a small or mid-size business (1–25 staff; service businesses, local businesses, consultancies, DTC brands). Knows marketing matters; has no time and no one owning it; has tried scattered freelancers or DIY and it stalled.
- **Awareness: Problem-Aware → Solution-Aware.** Homepage leads with problem + outcome ("grow without sacrificing the marketing"), then differentiates the solution kind (ONE operator owns the whole function) vs. agency / full-time hire / freelancers / DIY. Never lead with the offer.

## 1.3 Founder origin story (VOC + authority — real numbers, never inflate)

The founder spent **Jan 2025 – Jan 2026** qualifying small and mid-size businesses for **eBay's Start Up – Scale Up (SUSU) programme**, making 200–500 dials a day. Honest arithmetic: ~250 working days × 200–500 dials ≈ **50,000–125,000 dials** → phrase as **"tens of thousands of calls"** and **"conversations with thousands of business owners."**

**The repeated finding (the reason this service exists):** roughly **9 out of 10** of those businesses did little or no marketing — or did it half-heartedly. Not because they didn't care; because nobody owned it.

Approved phrasings:
- "I've spoken with thousands of business owners who knew marketing mattered — and just never got to it."
- "Nine out of ten businesses I spoke to were doing little or no marketing. Not because they didn't care — because nobody owned it."

## 1.4 O/CO objection table (the spine — counters placed at each point of friction)

| Objection | What they think | Counter (proof) | Placed where |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Trust** | "Can one person really replace a team/agency?" | Founder track record + named results + origin story + guarantee + real face/photo | Hero trust strip, About, testimonials |
| **Price** | "Is the subscription worth it?" | ROI vs agency retainer and vs full-time hire (UK marketing manager salary data); cost-of-inaction | Pricing, final CTA |
| **Fit** | "Will this work for *my* kind of business?" | Segmented proof, explicit "who this is for / not for", free intro call | Services, Work, FAQ |
| **Timing** | "Why now?" | The 9-in-10 finding; compounding cost of half-done marketing | Problem, Stakes, final CTA |
| **Effort** | "I don't have time to manage another contractor." | Done-for-you framing; "in the loop, not in the weeds"; 3-step onboarding | How-it-works, FAQ |
| **(implicit)** "Is a *VA* senior enough?" | — | CO-only: lead with seniority, outcomes, "felt like hiring a head of marketing" proof. Never name the fear. | Throughout |

## Copy rules of engagement

- PAS on the homepage hero; clarity beats cleverness; 7 Sweeps on everything (Clarity first, Zero last).
- Benefits = transformation ("marketing finally owned and moving"), never feature lists.
- Direct CTA repeated 3–4× per page, action verb, never "Submit".
- Never fabricate proof. Slots without real proof get `[TESTIMONIAL — collect]` placeholders and go on TODO-HUMAN.md. No fake logos, counts, ratings, or Review schema.
- Voice: energetic, direct, reassuring — "we understand" + "let's build". First person singular (it's one person; that's the product).
