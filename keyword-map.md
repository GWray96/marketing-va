## Explicitly NOT targeted

- "near me" / "[town]" terms — location-agnostic service, no location pages, no LocalBusiness schema.
- "how to market a small business" (KD 78) — head too hard; covered transitively by articles 7/8.
- "marketing executive" (1,900) as a money term — job-seeker intent; used only definitionally in article 14.
- "fractional cmo jobs" (170) — job intent.
