# Keyword Research — Raw Findings (DataForSEO, United Kingdom, English)

> Pulled live from DataForSEO Labs (Google), location: United Kingdom (2826), 2026-06-11.
> Columns: monthly search volume (UK) · CPC (£/$ as returned) · keyword difficulty (KD, 0–100 logarithmic) · main intent.
> Blank KD = not in difficulty index (typically very low competition). Volumes shown are latest 12-month averages.

## Headline findings

1. **"Marketing Executive VA" has zero recorded search volume.** Nobody searches the category name. What people search instead: **"fractional cmo" (880/mo, KD 10)**, **"outsourced marketing" (390/mo, KD 3)**, **"virtual marketing assistant" (90/mo)**, **"marketing va" (40/mo)**. Implication: the brand can *own* the name, but every page must also carry an established category term, and the homepage must define the entity in one extractable sentence for AEO.
2. **The outsourced-marketing cluster is shockingly low-difficulty.** KD 3–14 across the whole cluster with £10–29 CPCs — advertisers value the traffic highly but organic competition is weak. This is the site's primary commercial cluster.
3. **"seo marketing for small business" (720/mo, KD 6)** is the single best service-page opportunity found.
4. **"marketing plan template" (720/mo, KD 19)** and **"one page marketing plan" / "1 page marketing plan" (90+90/mo, KD 1–2)** map directly onto the chosen lead magnet. The lead-magnet LP should be *indexed*, not noindexed — it has real organic value.
5. **The AI cluster is high-volume but split:** generic heads are hard ("ai in marketing" 1,300/mo KD 35–56) while commercial long-tails are soft ("ai marketing tools" 480/mo KD 16; "ai marketing agency" 480/mo KD 2; "ai marketing automation" 140/mo KD 24). Target the long-tails with blog content.
6. **Seasonality caveat:** several outsourced-marketing terms show a September spike (2025-09) of 3–10× baseline — budget-planning season. Freshness-stamp and refresh those pages in late August.

## Method + caveats

- Tools: DataForSEO Labs `keyword_overview`, `keyword_suggestions` (seeds: outsourced marketing, fractional cmo, small business marketing, ai marketing), filtered vol > 10–30, UK/en.
- `keyword_ideas` (category-based) returned mostly irrelevant head terms (jobs, generic "digital marketing") and was discarded.
- Keywords marked "est." in keyword-map.md (e.g. "email marketing for small business", "ppc for small business") were not individually verified — reasoned from intent per playbook 2.1; verify before investing in those pages' content.
- Several clusters show a strong 2025-09 seasonal spike (budget season); plan content refreshes for late August.
